Shorts Factory

Shorts Factory is a personal tool used by a single person, the owner of the Google account that authorizes it. It runs in that person's own Google Colab notebook and prepares short video clips from video files the owner keeps in their own Google Drive.

The app requests access to Google Drive only to read the owner's source videos, save the finished clips and progress files, and read the owner's own settings spreadsheet. It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.

Privacy policy

Shorts Factory: личный инструмент владельца аккаунта Google для нарезки коротких клипов из его собственных видео на его Google Диске. Другим пользователям не предлагается.
