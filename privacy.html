Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026.

Shorts Factory (the "app") is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the Google account that authorizes it.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent the app uses the Google Drive scope to read video files and a settings spreadsheet in the owner's own Google Drive, and to save finished clips, logs and progress files back to the owner's Google Drive. It reads the owner's email address and storage quota only to confirm which account is signed in.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce video clips for the owner. The app runs in the owner's own Google Colab notebook. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models outside the owner's own workflow.

Storage and security

The access token is stored only in the owner's private Google Colab Secrets. It is never published, logged or stored in source code.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings: Security, Third-party apps and services. After that the app can no longer access Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
